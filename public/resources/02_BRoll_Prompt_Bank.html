How to Use This Bank

Three ways to get B-roll for your faceless video.

1. Generate it with AI

Use the prompts in this guide with Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Adobe Firefly, or Kling AI (for video). Best for custom or niche-specific visuals that stock footage can't provide.

2. Source it free

Pexels, Pixabay, Coverr, and Mixkit offer royalty-free video clips. Use the scene descriptions in this guide as your search terms.

3. Screen record it

For tech, software, or tutorial niches — screen recording IS your B-roll. No generation or sourcing needed.

Rule: B-roll should support what the narrator is saying, not distract from it. If a viewer looks at the screen and forgets to listen, the B-roll failed.
AI Generation Prompt Framework

Use this formula for every prompt you generate:

[SUBJECT] + [ACTION/STATE] + [SETTING] + [MOOD/LIGHTING] + [STYLE]

Example: "A focused professional woman reviewing financial charts on a laptop, modern home office, warm morning light, photorealistic, cinematic"
